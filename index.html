MidiControl

A Windows MIDI command librarian and patch preview tool with first-class Roland Integra-7 MIDI tone generator support.

Free Software

Command every patch from your desktop

Build organized lists of MIDI program-change, control-change, bank-change, and SysEx commands — then fire any one with a single click. 24 bookmarks hold up to 2,000 commands grouped by device, song, or setlist. Includes full Roland Integra-7 support with instant preview of 6,000+ tones.

  • 24 bookmarks · up to 2,000 MIDI commands per session
  • Supports bank change, patch change, control change, notes, and SysEx
  • Full Roland Integra-7 bundle — browse & preview 6,000+ tones
  • Works with any USB-MIDI or DIN-MIDI interface · 100% offline

🥁 MidiOrganize

Drag-and-drop MIDI loops from MidiOrganize to your DAW. No VST license. No internet. No CPU tax.

Free Software

Ditch the expensive drum VSTs

A standalone Windows MIDI loop librarian. Browse, preview, and drag MIDI loops into your DAW track — then route to a VST or external tone generator for superior sound with zero CPU overhead.

  • Drill-down loop browser — compatible with Groove Monkee and all MIDI libraries
  • Preview any loop before dragging to your DAW
  • Zero CPU overhead · no license key · fully offline
  • Route output to VST or external tone generator

🔀 MidiXLate Router

Highly configurable MIDI hardware router for complex studio and live-performance networks.

Custom Hardware

True MIDI routing, built in the Sierra Nevada

Unlike a MIDI hub, MidiXLate inspects every packet and routes it exactly where it needs to go. Define routing, filtering, translation, and channel rules from your PC — then the 84 MHz ARM Cortex-M3 handles everything in microseconds.

  • 7 MIDI In × 7 MIDI Out DIN ports + 1 USB-C
  • Filter by message type (Note, CC, PC, SysEx) per port
  • <1ms latency · 84 MHz ARM Cortex-M3 processor
  • Rent before you buy — $150/mo applied to $850 purchase
