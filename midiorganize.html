Ditch the expensive drum VSTs

MidiOrganize is a standalone Windows MIDI loop librarian. Browse, preview, and drag MIDI loops directly into your DAW track and route the track output to a VST. For superior sound, route the track output to an external tone generator. This has many benefits: zero CPU overhead, no license key, no activation server, and no required internet connectivity to use VST!

  • Familiar drill-down look and feel matching popular drum programs and VSTs
  • Preview any MIDI loop inside MidiOrganize before importing to your DAW project
  • Drag and drop MIDI loops directly into DAW MIDI tracks
  • Compatible with Groove Monkee free drum loops and all web MIDI libraries
  • Completely offline — no activation, no subscription, ever
  • Eliminates VST CPU latency and audio dropout issues
